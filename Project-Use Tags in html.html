WHERE IT BEGAN


Who invented Codingal?
Founded by Vivek Prakash and Satyam Baranwal, Codingal Education conducts live 1:1 classes for children from Grade 1-12 to learn coding. It is on a mission to inspire school kids to fall in love with coding. Its unique curriculum teaches coding scientifically by combining BIDE, STEAM and BLOOM's Taxonomy.
How old is Codingal?
Codingal is a seed company based in Dover (United States), founded in 2020 by Vivek Prakash and Satyam Baranwal. It operates as a Platform providing online coding classes for kids.Invented on: 12 Nov 2025
